Known, deliberately-not-fixed items

These are intentional behaviours or separate out-of-scope issues. Do not report them as bugs.

Horoscope Privacy Rule

Horoscope still requires an accepted interest even for Classic's combined reveal. This is an intentional privacy rule, not a bug. Flagged to product owner for possible future change.

Subscription Lookup Pattern Fragility

findTopByUserIdOrderByCreatedAtDesc lookup pattern exists in ~11 backend service methods beyond the fixed one (ChatService). If you hit a "false upgrade required" popup (especially after renewal), note the action/screen — this pattern is known fragile and not yet swept everywhere.

Hardcoded Pricing Page Mock Data

PremiumTab.tsx shows hardcoded mock data, not live DB prices/limits. Pricing page inaccuracies are a known separate, larger item.
